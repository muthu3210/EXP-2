# EXP-2
EXPT NO: 2 OCC AND LOAD CHARACTERISTICS OF D.C SHUNT GENERATOR

Aim:
To conduct load test on separately excited generators and to obtain the characteristics

Apparatus Required:

1	Volt meter	(0-300)V	MC	1

6	Connecting wires	-	-	As required

Fuse rating calculation for field and armature:

No load test

10 % of rated current (full load current)

Load test

125 % of rated current (full load current)

Precautions

1.   Motor side field rheostat should be kept at minimum resistance position.
2.   Generator side field rheostat should be kept at maximum resistance position.
3.   Starter should be in off position before switching on the supply.
4.   The DPST switch must be kept open.Procedure for open circuit test
Procedure
1.   Connections are given as per the circuit diagram.
2.   The motor is started with the help of THREE POINT starter.
3.   Adjust the motor speed to rated speed by adjusting motor field rheostat when the generator is disconnected from the load by DPST switch 2.
4.   By  varying  the  generator  field  rheostat  gradually,  the  open  circuit  voltage  [Eo]  and corresponding field current (If) are tabulated up to 125 % of rated voltage of generator.
5.   The motor is switched off by using DPST switch 1 after bringing all the rheostats to initial position.

Procedure for Load test:

1.   Connections are given as per the circuit diagram
2.   The prime mover is started with the help of three point starter and it is made to run at rated speed when the generator is disconnected from the load by DPST switch 2.
3.   By varying the generator field rheostat gradually, the rated voltage [Eg] is obtained.
4.   The ammeter and voltmeter readings are observed at no load condition.
5.   The ammeter and voltmeter readings are observed for different loads up to the rated current by closing the DPST switch 2.
6.   After tabulating all the readings the load is brought to its initial position.
7.   The motor is switched off by using DPST switch 1 after bringing all the rheostats to initial position.

CALCULATIONS
1. OPEN CIRCUIT CHARACTERISTICS (OCC)
Given Data:

Minimum field current, If(min) = 0 A

Maximum field current, If(max) = 0.30 A

Minimum generated voltage, E₀(min) = 0 V

Maximum generated voltage, E₀(max) = 142 V

Calculation 1: Change in Generated EMF

Change in generated EMF,

ΔE₀ = E₀(max) − E₀(min)

ΔE₀ = 142 − 0

ΔE₀ = 142 V

Calculation 2: Change in Field Current

Change in field current,

ΔIf = If(max) − If(min)

ΔIf = 0.30 − 0

ΔIf = 0.30 A

Calculation 3: Average Rate of Increase in Generated EMF

Average rate of increase = Change in EMF / Change in field current

= 142 / 0.30

Average rate of increase = 473.33 V/A

Note: This is the average change over the recorded range, not the slope at every point of the OCC curve.

2. LOAD CHARACTERISTICS
Given Data:

Initial recorded load current, IL₁ = 1.2 A

Final recorded load current, IL₂ = 12.7 A

Initial recorded terminal voltage, V₁ = 220 V

Final recorded terminal voltage, V₂ = 176 V

Calculation 1: Decrease in Terminal Voltage

Decrease in terminal voltage,

ΔV = V₁ − V₂

ΔV = 220 − 176

ΔV = 44 V

Calculation 2: Percentage Decrease in Terminal Voltage

Percentage decrease in voltage,

= [(V₁ − V₂) / V₁] × 100

= [(220 − 176) / 220] × 100

= (44 / 220) × 100

Percentage decrease in voltage = 20%

Calculation 3: Change in Load Current

Change in load current,

ΔIL = IL₂ − IL₁

ΔIL = 12.7 − 1.2

ΔIL = 11.5 A

Calculation 4: Average Decrease in Terminal Voltage per Ampere

Average voltage decrease per ampere,

= Change in terminal voltage / Change in load current

= 44 / 11.5

Average voltage decrease = 3.83 V/A

Note: This is the average change between the first and last tabulated readings.

Calculation 5: Output Power at the Final Load Reading

Output power, P = V × I

Final terminal voltage, V = 176 V

Final load current, IL = 12.7 A

P = 176 × 12.7

P = 2235.2 W

Output power = 2.2352 kW

3. VOLTAGE REGULATION
The reference image shows a voltmeter reading of 225 V before the load-test observation table. Using this as the no-load voltage and 176 V as the final loaded voltage:

No-load voltage, V₀ = 225 V

Full-load test voltage, VFL = 176 V

Voltage regulation,

= [(V₀ − VFL) / VFL] × 100

= [(225 − 176) / 176] × 100

= (49 / 176) × 100

Voltage regulation = 27.84%

Note: This calculation assumes that the 225 V meter reading represents the no-load voltage at the same operating speed. Confirm this with your laboratory instructor before including it in the final record.

4. FUSE RATING CALCULATION
Let the rated full-load current of the generator be IFL.

For the no-load test,

Fuse current = 0.10 × IFL

For the load test,

Fuse current = 1.25 × IFL

The rated full-load current is not provided in the reference images. Therefore, the numerical fuse ratings cannot be determined reliably without the generator nameplate value or the value specified by the laboratory instructor.
